Two directions

Staying and selling are both legitimate answers

Families usually arrive here because the house holds most of the household's equity, and a decision about that equity feels overdue. The useful work is to compare, not to rush.

Staying, with a possible reverse mortgage

  • The goal is to remain in the home you already have, if that is still a good daily fit.
  • A reverse mortgage is one way some eligible homeowners draw on equity without selling. The details belong with a lender.
  • I can also introduce in-home caregivers, or a senior placement advisor, if help at home or a community would serve better than a loan.
  • If staying looks like the stronger financial decision, I will say so.

Selling, and moving on a measured timeline

  • The goal is a well-planned move, not a hasty listing, when the house no longer supports daily life.
  • I personally coordinate the outreach, the auction or estate sale, the cleanout and the unpacking at the new place.
  • Most of this work takes 6 to 12 months from the first conversation to the first night in the new home.
  • If a sale looks like the stronger path, I will say that too, and we will plan it at a pace the household can live with.

Reverse Lending Partners

When a reverse mortgage deserves a real look, I make the introduction and step back. They can explain how the loan works, what it costs, and whether a household is likely to qualify. I stay available for the real-estate side of the decision: what the home might sell for, what a move would involve, and whether selling would actually be clearer.

Why this question comes up

The house, the weather, and the life you wanted next

People do not usually begin with a loan product. They begin with a feeling that the house, or the budget, is asking a new question.

The winters and the upkeep

Heating bills, ice and a yard that has become a job can make a home feel heavier than it used to, even when no one wants to leave it.

Wanting family close

Some people want to stay in town to be near the kids and grandkids. A move is not the only way to make that work.

The retirement home they worked for

If this is the house they planned to grow old in, giving it up should be a choice, not a default. A reverse mortgage is one way some people try to stay.

Needing the equity, not a new address

Sometimes the need is cash or a more comfortable monthly picture, and the rooms themselves still fit. That is worth naming plainly, before anyone calls a mover.

If selling is the better path

I will still handle the move, not just the listing

A reverse mortgage is not always the answer, and I will not pretend otherwise. When a sale is clearer, I walk the household through a 6 to 12 month plan and I personally coordinate the work that usually stalls a family.

That includes the outreach to the people who help with auction and cleanout, often Golden Gavel for an estate sale, and unpacking help at the new place so the first night feels like home.

  1. Month 1

    A conversation first

    Reasons, timing, and a plain comparison of staying versus selling. No paperwork and no loan application.

  2. Month 1–2

    A home assessment

    A walk-through and an honest look at what the house could sell for, so the equity conversation has real numbers on the real-estate side.

  3. Months 2–5

    Sorting

    Keep, give to family, sell, donate. One room at a time.

  4. Months 3–6

    Auction and estate sale

    I coordinate an auction or estate sale for what should not simply be boxed or discarded.

  5. Months 4–7

    Cleanout and junk removal

    I line up the crew that clears what is left, so the house is empty and ready.

  6. Months 5–8

    Prep, staging and photos

    Small repairs and staging where it actually helps, then a sale timeline that follows the move.

  7. Moving week

    Unpacking at the new place

    I help set up the new home, so the last box is not the family's to figure out alone.

Common questions

What people usually ask before they call

Are you a reverse mortgage lender?

No. I am a real estate agent with KW Legacy Partners. I do not originate loans, quote rates or decide eligibility. For those details I introduce families to Reverse Lending Partners.

What is a reverse mortgage, in plain terms?

In simple terms, it is a loan that can let an eligible homeowner draw on home equity while staying in the home. The rules, the costs and whether it fits a particular household are questions for a lender. Reverse Lending Partners can walk through those details with you.

When might selling be the better path?

When the house, the upkeep or the location no longer supports daily life, or when a move would better serve the family's plans. If I think a sale is the stronger financial decision, I will say so, and I can personally coordinate a 6 to 12 month downsizing plan.

Will you tell us what we should do?

I will compare the paths honestly, but I do not give legal, tax or financial advice. Lending questions go to Reverse Lending Partners. The decision stays with the homeowner and the family.

Is there a cost to have this conversation?

No. The first conversation is simply a conversation, and there is no obligation to sell or to apply for a loan.
